ABOUT THE PROJECT

Rome in Two Takes

Rome in Two Takes was developed as the final project for the Information Modeling and Web Technologies course within the Master's Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna.

The project explores Rome through cinema, combining film locations, digital itineraries and web design. Six films were selected to represent different historical periods and perspectives on the city, and their locations were organized into two complementary itineraries: Historical Journey and Women & Urban Space.

The overall concept of the website, the selection of the films, the definition of the two itineraries and the base visual identity were developed collaboratively by the whole team.
