Rome in Two Takes

Discover Rome through cinema

Rome is more than a backdrop for cinema. Across different decades, its streets, neighborhoods, monuments and domestic spaces have reflected political changes, social transformations and individual experiences.

Rome in Two Takes offers two itineraries through real film locations: one follows the historical evolution of twentieth-century Rome, while the other explores the relationship between women, emancipation and urban space.
